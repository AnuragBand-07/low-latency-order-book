Low-Latency Order Book Matching Engine

A short walkthrough of price-time priority matching: a resting bid, a cross that prints at the maker’s price, a short market simulation, and an in-browser timing run. The C++ engine uses the same rules and measures 4.3M addOrder/sec with a 625 ns p99.

Transcript

This is a C++ limit order book. Orders follow price-time priority. A buy that does not cross the ask rests in a FIFO queue. A sell that crosses the best bid trades at the maker’s price. The native engine sustains 4.3 million adds per second, with a p99 latency of 625 nanoseconds.
